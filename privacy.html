Information we may collect When you submit a Request a Quote or other enquiry, we may receive information such as: name and company name; country and destination country; business email address and phone/WhatsApp number; buyer or business type; product required and quantity; preferred commercial terms and purchase frequency; product specifications, packaging requirements and other comments; documents, images or reference files that you voluntarily upload; and information contained in subsequent business communications with us. Why we use enquiry information We may use information submitted through the website to: understand and respond to sourcing enquiries; identify potential products and suppliers in India; prepare or communicate quotations and commercial proposals; discuss product specifications, packaging, documentation and shipment requirements; communicate with prospective or existing business customers; coordinate legitimate business activities with suppliers, service providers and logistics partners where necessary; and maintain records, prevent misuse and protect the security and integrity of our services. We seek to collect and use information that is reasonably relevant to these purposes and do not use enquiry information for unrelated purposes except where permitted or required by applicable law. 3. Consent and your choices Where consent is relied upon as the legal basis for processing personal data, we intend to obtain clear affirmative consent for the specified purpose and provide appropriate information about that processing. Where applicable law gives you a right to withdraw consent, you may contact us using the details below. Withdrawal does not necessarily affect processing that is permitted or required on another lawful basis. 4. Sharing and service providers We may use third-party service providers to operate the website and enquiry process, including website hosting, form processing, email and security services. We may also share relevant enquiry information with suppliers, logistics providers, testing or documentation providers, professional advisers or other business partners where reasonably necessary to evaluate, quote for or fulfil a legitimate sourcing requirement. We do not sell personal information submitted through the website as a standalone commercial data product. 5.
