01 / WHO WE ARE

Connecting India's products with global markets.

ANNACH EXPORTS, a business initiative of Annach Ventures Global LLP, is an India-based merchant exporter and sourcing partner connecting international buyers with agricultural, food and specialty products from India.

We work with selected suppliers and producers to identify products that align with buyer requirements for quality, specifications, packaging, quantity and applicable export documentation.

Our objective is simple: make sourcing from India easier, more transparent and more dependable.
